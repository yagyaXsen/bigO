# bigO

> **A small, focused digital studio building modern websites, web apps, and digital experiences that are fast, secure, and built to last.**

bigO is a digital studio focused on helping businesses build and grow their digital presence.

We combine **development, design, and digital growth** under one small team. Instead of large agency structures, clients work directly with the people designing, building, and managing their projects.

No unnecessary layers. No complicated processes.

## How We Work

Our typical workflow is simple:

```text
Discovery
   ↓
Research & Planning
   ↓
Design
   ↓
Development
   ↓
Testing
   ↓
Launch
   ↓
Maintenance & Growth
```

### Discovery

We understand the business, audience, goals, competitors, and requirements.

### Design

We create the structure, visual direction, and user experience before development.

### Development

The approved design is converted into a fast, responsive, production-ready application.

### Testing

We test across devices, browsers, forms, integrations, performance, and security.

### Launch

We configure hosting, domains, SSL, analytics, and production deployment.

### Growth

After launch, we can continue improving the website through maintenance, SEO, content, analytics, and marketing.
