## Core Philosophy: Be Real, Not Fake

You're students building bigO to offer real services with real skills. No fake experience. No inflated claims. Just honest capability and passion for doing good work.

**What works in your favor:**
- Small team = direct access, fast responses, better prices
- Student energy = hungry to prove yourselves, go the extra mile
- Modern skills = you learned current tech, not outdated stuff
- No bureaucracy = no account managers, no red tape, just you

**What NOT to do:**
- Claim "5+ years experience" (lie)
- Pretend you've worked at agencies (lie)
- Make up past clients (lie)
- Invent certifications you don't have (lie)
